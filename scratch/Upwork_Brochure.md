# Premium EdTech & Mock Test Platform
## Complete Mobile App & Web Solution for Coaching Institutes

Thank you for viewing my project catalog! This document outlines exactly what is included in the platform I will deploy for you.

### 🌟 Core Platform Features
*   **Cross-Platform Mobile App:** Built with Flutter. Runs flawlessly on both Android and iOS devices.
*   **Responsive Web Portal:** A beautiful React/Next.js web dashboard where students can take exams on their laptops or desktop computers.
*   **Secure Admin Panel:** Manage your students, upload exams, and view analytics from a secure backend.

### 📝 Advanced Exam Engine
*   **Timed Exams:** Set exact durations for mock tests. Auto-submits when the timer runs out.
*   **Negative Marking:** Fully configurable scoring algorithms to mimic real competitive exams (e.g., State Police, SSC, UPSC).
*   **Instant Analytics:** Students get immediate feedback, detailed solutions, and a "State/Platform Rank" to compare themselves against others.
*   **Save & Resume:** State is saved securely. If a student loses internet connection, they do not lose their progress.

### 📚 Study Materials & Monetization
*   **E-Book/PDF Notes Distribution:** Securely upload notes and study materials for your students.
*   **Payment Gateway Ready:** Razorpay integration is already built-in. Start selling your premium test series on day one.
*   **Ad Monetization:** Seamless integration with Google AdSense (Web) and AdMob (Mobile) for additional revenue streams.

### 🏃‍♂️ Unique Add-ons
*   **Physical Fitness Tracker:** A unique module that allows students preparing for defense/police exams to log their running times and physical metrics.
